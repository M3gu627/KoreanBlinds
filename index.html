About LEI-LO

우리의 이야기

Founded in Seoul, LEI-LO brings the serene beauty of Korean tradition into modern homes worldwide. Inspired by cherry blossoms in spring and the elegant simplicity of hanok windows, we craft blinds that are more than functional — they are art.

Our Mission

To bring peace, beauty, and Korean elegance into every home through handcrafted window solutions.

Our Vision

To become the global symbol of premium Korean design — where every window tells a story.

Our Journey

Established in 2018 in Seoul. Today, LEI-LO is loved in over 20 countries.
