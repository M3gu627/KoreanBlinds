About LEI-LO

Preserving Korean Heritage Through Excellence

Our Story

Founded with a passion for Korean traditional craftsmanship, LEI-LO has been bringing the timeless beauty of Korean design into modern homes and offices. Our name represents the harmony between light (Lei) and flow (Lo), reflecting our philosophy of creating spaces that breathe with natural elegance.

We believe that window treatments are more than just functional items – they are expressions of cultural identity and personal style. Each blind we create carries the essence of Korean heritage, meticulously crafted to meet contemporary needs while honoring traditional techniques.
